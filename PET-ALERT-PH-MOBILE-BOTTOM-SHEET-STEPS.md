# Pet Alert PH Mobile Bottom Sheet Update

This frontend-only update keeps the map visible on phones by placing the report list inside a draggable bottom sheet.

## Mobile behavior
- Drag the handle upward to show more reports.
- Drag it downward to reveal more of the map.
- Tap a report to center the map and open its details.
- Tap a map pin to open its report.
- Search remains inside the sheet.
- Terms & Safety becomes a floating round button on phones.

No Supabase SQL, environment variables, or new API keys are required.
